Privacy Policy

DevLocalize Pro · Effective date: September 28, 2026

At a glance

  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no ads, no trackers and no user accounts.
  • Your project files are processed on your own computer.
  • Text is sent to an AI provider only when you choose to use AI translation, using your own API key.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Microsoft. We never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

DevLocalize Pro ("the app") is a Windows desktop application for managing and translating software localization files, developed and published by Nechirvan Azzo ("we", "us").

2. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your computer, in the app's private storage provided by Windows, and is never sent to us:

  • Project files you open (JSON, RESX, ARB, YAML, XML), read and written only in the folder you choose.
  • Recently opened project folders.
  • Settings (language, theme, glossary, style guide, AI model).
  • Translation memory (your previously approved translations).
  • AI provider API keys, stored in Windows Credential Locker (encrypted by Windows).

If you enable "Detect outdated translations", the app uses the Git program installed on your computer to read your project history. This happens entirely on your device.

All of this data is removed when you uninstall the app. You can also clear the translation memory or remove API keys at any time in Settings.

3. AI translation

AI translation is optional and runs only when you start it. When you do:

  • The app sends the text of the strings being translated to the AI provider you selected (Anthropic or OpenAI), together with translator comments for those strings, your glossary, your style guide, and the language codes.
  • The request goes directly from your computer to the provider using your own API key. It does not pass through our servers and we cannot see it.
  • Your full project files and file paths are not sent.

The provider processes this text under its own terms and privacy policy: Anthropic · OpenAI.

Do not use AI translation on strings containing confidential information you are not permitted to share with a third-party service.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and managed through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft processes your payment and tells the app only whether your subscription is active and when it expires. We do not receive your name, payment card or billing address. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Microsoft account.

5. Diagnostics

The app contains no analytics or tracking code. If the app crashes, Windows may share anonymous crash and performance reports with us through Microsoft Partner Center, depending on your Windows diagnostic data settings.

6. Command-line tool (CLI)

If you request a CLI license key, you send us your email address so we can issue and deliver the key. The key contains your email address, license type and expiry date so it can be verified offline. We use your email only to issue, renew and support your license.

7. Permissions

  • Internet access: to contact your chosen AI provider and check your subscription with the Microsoft Store.
  • Files you select: the app only accesses folders and files you choose.

8. Your choices and rights

You may remove API keys or clear the translation memory in Settings, stop using AI translation, or uninstall the app to delete all local app data. You may also ask us to access or delete any email or CLI license information we hold about you.

9. Children

The app is a professional tool for developers and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its date.
